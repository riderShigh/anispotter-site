Coordinate Fuzzing

AniSpotter's public map displays only fuzzed (coarsened) location coordinates. Exact coordinates are stored server-side but never shown to other users.

Sensitive Species

Sensitive or protected species may be further coarsened or hidden from the public map to protect wildlife.

Home Privacy Zone

You can define a home privacy zone on your device. Sightings within this zone:

Service Quality

AniSpotter makes no guarantee that identifications are accurate. Always verify results and consult wildlife experts or field guides for critical decisions.
